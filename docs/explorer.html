Refusal direction explorer

Interactive view of the result files of refusal-direction-replication (Arditi et al. 2024, replicated on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct and Gemma-2-2b-it). Every chart is drawn from a CSV or JSONL in results/<model>/; nothing is computed here beyond reading those files. Hover for values, drag to zoom, double-click to reset. Each chart has a table view.
